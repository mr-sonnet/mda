A vision for Whitehaven

The Othmaniyah
Islamic Campus.

A 5.4-acre community campus inspired by the kulliye tradition: faith at the center, practical service all around it.

The idea

More than a building. An ecosystem of care.

A kulliye is a group of buildings centered around a mosque and dedicated to social good. Historically, these campuses included schools, kitchens, clinics, hospitality, and other charitable services.

Memphis Dawah Association is bringing that model to Whitehaven through spaces for prayer, education, a farmers market, food relief, health initiatives, radio, and community gathering.

The Chinar symbol

Built for generations.

Known for longevity, beauty, strength, and a wide canopy, the Chinar tree represents an educational endeavor meant to offer shelter, stillness, growth, and nourishment for generations.
