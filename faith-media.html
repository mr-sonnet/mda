Public responsibility

Education counters extremism.

We believe careful education is among the strongest responses to radicalization and misinformation. Authentic teaching helps people identify aberrant ideas, understand Islam’s emphasis on family, neighbors, learning, productive citizenship, and build resilient communities.

Our outreach seeks common ground with people of goodwill and offers direction, purpose, and practical collaboration.
