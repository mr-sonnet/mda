How it works

Neighbors helping neighbors.

Our food pantry serves more than 300 families in a typical week. We focus on dependable access, respectful service, and nourishing food for households in and around Whitehaven.

You can help in four ways: volunteer at a distribution, donate to sustain food purchases, advocate for food security, or share the program with someone who needs it.
