Program overview

Capital for growers. Benefits for the whole community.

Memphis Dawah Association, in partnership with USDA and TIME Inc., created a $200,000 microgrant initiative for 25 small-scale growers across Shelby County. Individual awards of up to $8,000 were designed to help producers expand, purchase supplies, improve soil health, and adopt climate-resilient practices.

The 2025 application period is now closed. This page remains available as a public record of the program model, eligibility, funding uses, and review process. Future opportunities will be posted when dates and funding are confirmed.

How it worked

Application and review process.

A structured process connected local screening, site review, partner oversight, and post-award accountability.

  1. 01

    Application

    Growers submitted their project information for the active funding cycle.

  2. 02

    Committee review

    A screening committee evaluated applications and visited shortlisted projects.

  3. 03

    Partner review

    The approval chain included program and agriculture partners identified for the initiative.

  4. 04

    Award notification

    Selected growers received funding decisions and project expectations.

  5. 05

    Verification

    Awardees documented spending and progress through receipts, photos, training, and reporting.

Common questions

Program FAQ.

Can individuals apply?

The 2025 cycle accepted individuals and small-scale producers located in Shelby County, Tennessee.

Could equipment be purchased?

Yes. Equipment was an eligible use, subject to the program's funding rules and approved project budget.

Were youth eligible?

Youth grants were available. Applicants under 18 required guardian consent.

Was a Farm Record Number required?

No. The published 2025 program information stated that an FSA Farm Record Number was not required.

Are applications currently open?

No. The 2025 application cycle is closed. Future opportunities will be announced when confirmed.
