# Memphis Dawah Association content migration

## Coverage

The rebuilt website preserves every destination identified in the supplied scrape and now adds a dedicated `microgrant.html` page based on the current MDA Microgrant Program information.

The site includes organization history, the two properties, education, religious resources, food access, The Suq, health, legal resources, partners, workforce and STEM program descriptions, microgrant information, media, statements, news, events, volunteering, contact, and donation.

## Information architecture

The content is grouped into five areas:

1. Local community services and program impact
2. Organization and two Memphis properties
3. Education and worship information
4. Media, statements, news, and archived content
5. Partnership, participation, and giving

## Preservation safeguards

- All 327 non-empty paragraphs extracted from the supplied Word scrape remain in `source-archive.html`.
- The live website has no active links, images, scripts, or styles loaded from the retired Hostinger site.
- Incomplete source items are listed locally at `missing-content.html`.
- Historical items and closed funding cycles are labeled so they cannot be mistaken for current opportunities.
- Traditional health content includes a medical disclaimer.
