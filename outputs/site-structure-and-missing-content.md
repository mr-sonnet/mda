# New website structure

The old navigation content has been retained and reorganized around MDA's strongest public story: local service, education, two Memphis properties, community partnerships, and sustainable support.

## Current homepage order

1. Community-service hero with partnership calls to action
2. Public impact figures
3. Kulliye service mission
4. Eight-program service portfolio
5. MDA Microgrant Program feature
6. Public-agency and funding-partner section
7. Two-property overview
8. Donation and roof-replacement support
9. Partnership contact call to action

Jumu'ah and prayer times are no longer homepage centerpieces. The separate prayer-information page states that Jumu'ah is temporarily paused because of plumbing and electrical issues.

## Conversion and credibility improvements

- Services and outcomes appear before the property and donation sections.
- Food access, workforce development, STEM, urban agriculture, health, education, media, and resource navigation are presented as one connected portfolio.
- A dedicated section speaks directly to public agencies, funders, schools, nonprofits, businesses, and community partners.
- The Microgrant Program page preserves the $200,000 initiative, 25-grower reach, up-to-$8,000 awards, eligibility, permitted uses, review steps, accountability requirements, and current closed status.
- Donation remains visible in the header and persistent support bar and is connected to both programs and property preservation.
- Three one-click color themes remain available, with Forest as the default.
- Navigation dropdowns use a modern animated chevron, desktop hover access, and mobile tap access.
