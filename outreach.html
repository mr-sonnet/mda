The Origin of Humoral Medicine

The source presents humoral medicine as a historical medical framework based on four natures—hotness, coldness, wetness, and dryness—and four humors. It connects the tradition to narratives about Prophetic knowledge and discusses pulse, diet, blood, and disease within that framework.

The article argues that imbalance in food and humors causes illness and references the historical work of Swedish physician Robin Fåhræus in connection with blood sedimentation. Modern medicine does not validate the article’s broad claims in the form presented.
