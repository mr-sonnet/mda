The Suq is a marketplace and social gathering place supporting local farmers, producers, artisans, and neighbors.

Our mission

A market made for community.

The Whitehaven Kulliye Farmers Market creates a welcoming marketplace for locally grown food, handmade goods, and neighborhood relationships.

The Suq is officially registered with the USDA National Farmers Market Directory. Vendors and community partners are invited to connect with us about participation.
